# Landing page: Barline

Built in the app at `/welcome` (src/screens/Welcome.tsx). Copy in the brand voice.

1. **Hero.** "Your gym log, with nothing locked." Under it: "Log every set,
   rest on a timer, see the line go up. Every routine, every exercise, every
   chart: free." Button: **Start logging** (opens the app, no sign-up).
   Screenshot: the live workout (clone-screens/S05.png).
2. **The problem** (paraphrased, no reviewer quotes): popular lifting logs cap
   routines and custom exercises on their free plan, and hide your long-term
   charts behind a subscription.
3. **How it works.** 1) Start a workout or a routine. 2) Tick each set; the
   rest timer starts. 3) Save; records and charts update.
4. **Features, fixes first.** No caps on routines or custom exercises. All-time
   charts. No account, data on your phone, CSV export. Then parity: supersets,
   set types, RPE, PR detection, body weight.
5. **Pricing.** Table from pricing.md.
6. **FAQ.** Is it really free? (yes, the log is; Sync is the paid part, when
   it ships). Do I need an account? (no). Can I get my data out? (CSV, any
   time). Can I import from other apps? (not yet: importer is on the list).
   Does it work offline? (yes).
7. **Final call to action.** "Start logging. It's already on your phone."

No testimonials, user counts, ratings or press logos: there are none yet.
